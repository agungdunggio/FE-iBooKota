# iBooKota Landing Page — Short PRD

## 1. Product

iBooKota is an internal office meeting-room booking assistant. Employees use WhatsApp to ask about room availability and make bookings. AI interprets the request, checks availability, records the booking in the database, and syncs it with Google Calendar.

## 2. Target user

**Primary:** Employees booking meeting rooms during the workday.

They want to:
- Find an available room quickly.
- Ask naturally, without learning another interface.
- Avoid double bookings.
- Receive clear booking confirmation.

**Secondary:** Office, facilities, and IT teams who need reliable booking records.

## 3. Value proposition

> **Book the right meeting room in one WhatsApp conversation.**

iBooKota removes manual coordination. Employees ask what is available, AI checks the schedule, confirms the booking, and keeps the database and Google Calendar aligned.

## 4. Primary CTA

**Book via WhatsApp**

The CTA opens the organization’s WhatsApp booking contact.

Optional secondary link: **See how it works**.

## 5. Required landing-page sections

### Hero

**Headline:** Book a meeting room in one WhatsApp conversation.

**Supporting copy:** Ask iBooKota what’s available. It checks the schedule, confirms your booking, and syncs it automatically.

**CTA:** `Book via WhatsApp`

### How it works

1. **Ask** — Send a WhatsApp message describing your meeting.
2. **Check** — iBooKota’s AI finds available rooms and times.
3. **Confirm** — Your booking is recorded and synced with Google Calendar.

### Example conversation

- Employee: “I need a room for six people tomorrow at 2 PM.”
- iBooKota: “Room Merapi is available from 2–3 PM. Should I book it?”
- Employee: “Yes.”
- iBooKota: “Booked. The event is on Google Calendar.”

### Why iBooKota

- No new booking system to learn.
- Ask questions in natural language.
- Fewer scheduling conflicts.
- Booking records stay up to date.

### Integration reassurance

> WhatsApp for conversation.  
> AI for understanding.  
> Database for reliable records.  
> Google Calendar for shared visibility.

### FAQ

- Who can use iBooKota?
- How do I book a room?
- Can I ask which rooms are available?
- Where does the confirmed booking appear?
- What happens if no room is available?

### Final CTA

> Ready to book a room?

`Book via WhatsApp`

## 6. Tone of voice

**Understated, clear, calm, and useful.**

Use short sentences, plain workplace language, specific benefits, and confident but restrained claims. Avoid buzzwords, overexplaining the AI, humor that weakens trust, and promises outside the core flow.

## 7. MVP scope

### Included

- Employee-focused landing page.
- WhatsApp-first booking CTA.
- AI availability and booking explanation.
- Database record explanation.
- Google Calendar sync explanation.
- Example conversation.
- Basic FAQ.

### Not included

- Admin dashboard messaging.
- Utilization analytics.
- Room recommendations by equipment or location.
- Web-based booking flow.
- Pricing or public signup.

## 8. Success criteria

The page succeeds when an employee can, within seconds:

1. Understand what iBooKota does.
2. Understand that booking happens through WhatsApp.
3. Trust that availability and calendar records stay synchronized.
4. Find and click **Book via WhatsApp**.

**Deliberate simplification:** Admin and analytics messaging is excluded; add it when the page must sell to facilities or leadership too.
