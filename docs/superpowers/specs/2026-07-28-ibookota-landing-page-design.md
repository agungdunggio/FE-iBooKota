# iBooKota Landing Page Design / PRD

## Product

iBooKota is an internal office meeting-room booking assistant. Employees use WhatsApp to ask about room availability and make bookings. AI interprets the request, checks availability, records the booking in the database, and syncs it with Google Calendar.

## Target user

Primary audience: employees booking meeting rooms during the workday.

They need to find an available room quickly, ask naturally without learning another interface, avoid double bookings, and receive clear confirmation.

Secondary audience: office, facilities, and IT teams who need reliable booking records.

## Value proposition

> **Book the right meeting room in one WhatsApp conversation.**

Employees ask what is available. iBooKota checks the schedule, confirms the booking, and keeps the database and Google Calendar aligned.

## Primary action

`Book via WhatsApp` opens the organization’s WhatsApp booking contact.

Optional secondary action: `See how it works`.

## Required sections

1. **Hero** — Headline: “Book a meeting room in one WhatsApp conversation.” Supporting copy explains that iBooKota checks availability, confirms the booking, and syncs it automatically. Include the primary CTA.
2. **How it works** — Three steps: Ask, Check, Confirm.
3. **Example conversation** — Show an employee asking for a room, iBooKota offering an available room, and the confirmation appearing on Google Calendar.
4. **Why iBooKota** — No new system to learn; natural-language questions; fewer conflicts; current booking records.
5. **Integration reassurance** — WhatsApp for conversation, AI for understanding, database for records, Google Calendar for visibility.
6. **FAQ** — Access, booking flow, availability questions, confirmation location, and no-room-available behavior.
7. **Final CTA** — “Ready to book a room?” with `Book via WhatsApp`.

## Tone of voice

Understated, clear, calm, and useful. Use short sentences, plain workplace language, specific benefits, and restrained claims. Avoid buzzwords, excessive AI explanation, distracting humor, and promises outside the core flow.

## MVP scope

Included: employee-focused page, WhatsApp-first CTA, AI booking explanation, database and Google Calendar sync explanation, example conversation, and basic FAQ.

Excluded: admin dashboard, utilization analytics, equipment/location recommendations, web booking, pricing, and public signup.

## Success criteria

An employee can within seconds:

- Understand what iBooKota does.
- Understand that booking happens through WhatsApp.
- Trust that availability and calendar records stay synchronized.
- Find and click `Book via WhatsApp`.

## Deliberate simplification

Admin and analytics messaging is excluded. Add it when the page must persuade facilities or leadership as a primary audience.
